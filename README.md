## 🚀 Team Projects

### OneUni — DevDash'26 Hackathon
**404 Team Not Found | Team Leader & QA**

OneUni is a unified campus platform developed during the DevDash'26 Hackathon to bring essential student and staff services into a single digital platform.

The platform provides access to:
- 📢 Announcements
- 📅 Events and campus activities
- 📚 Academic support
- 🏢 Facilities and services
- ❓ FAQs
- 🔔 Notifications
- 📅 Bookings and service requests
- 🤖 AI-powered assistance
- 👥 Role-based student and staff features

**My Contributions:**
- 👩‍💼 **Team Leadership** — Coordinated the team, delegated tasks, monitored progress, and helped keep development aligned with the project requirements and hackathon timeline.
- 🧪 **Testing & Quality Assurance** — Tested the application features and user flows, identified issues, verified fixes, and supported the overall quality of the final product.
- 📄 **Project Report** — Prepared and contributed to the final project documentation, including the project overview, development process, testing, and supporting documentation.
- 🎤 **Presentation** — Prepared and delivered the project presentation, explaining the problem, proposed solution, key features, development approach, and project outcome.

**Technologies:**
`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Firebase` `Tailwind CSS`
